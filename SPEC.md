PRODUCT VISION

HZ Chord AI is a next-generation AI-powered music analysis, audio engineering, transcription, and music production platform designed specifically for musicians, producers, worship teams, music teachers, students, guitarists, keyboard players, and audio engineers.

The goal is to create the most advanced mobile music analysis application available on Android by combining the capabilities of a professional chord detector, arpeggio detector, BPM analyzer, tuner, music theory assistant, AI stem separator, transcription engine, phrase recognition system, audio restoration suite, and mobile music workstation into a single application.

The application must work completely offline, performing all audio analysis, machine learning inference, chord recognition, stem separation, transcription, and music processing directly on the user's device without requiring cloud services.

ARPEGGIO, LICK AND SUNGURA DETECTION ENGINE

Unlike traditional chord detectors, HZ Chord AI must understand music even when notes are played one at a time.

The application should continuously analyze:
- Arpeggios
- Single-note phrases
- Implied chords
- Lead guitar runs
- Bass walks
- Melodic phrases
- Guitar licks
- Scale runs

Special optimization should be provided for:

- Jit

- Rhumba
